**Title:** I got tired of 2014-era spreadsheets, so I built a free, modern 3D Print Cost & Quote Calculator (FDM + Resin)

**Body:**

Hey everyone,

If you sell prints on Etsy, do local fabrication jobs, or just want to know how much that 48-hour helmet print *actually* cost you, you’ve probably noticed the pricing tools out there are… rough. Most are either clunky Excel sheets, locked behind subscriptions, or 10-year-old WordPress calculators that break on mobile and don't even support resin.

Most people just calculate `spool cost / grams used` and stop there. But if you don't account for electricity spikes, nozzle/FEP wear, prep/sanding time, and failure margin, you’re essentially paying clients to take your prints.

To scratch my own itch, I built a fast, free web tool: **[Link to 3d-print-cost-calculator]**

### What it factors in:
* **FDM & Resin Presets:** Density & spool/bottle costs for PLA, PETG, ABS, TPU, and UV Resin (plus custom inputs).
* **True Electricity Usage:** Watts × print duration × your local kWh rate (especially relevant in high-energy regions).
* **Machine Depreciation:** A flat hourly rate or percentage buffer for maintenance, nozzle replacements, and machine wear.
* **Hands-on Labor:** Separate billable rates for slicing/file prep and manual post-processing (support removal, curing, sanding).
* **Profit Margin & Markup:** Configurable markup percentages so you actually make a margin on custom work.
* **Instant Quote Card:** Generates a clean, client-ready summary breakdown you can save or print on the spot.

It’s completely free, mobile-friendly, and requires no login or sign-up.
